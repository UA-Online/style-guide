---
title: seasons
section: Grammar/Punctuation
---
Do not capitalize seasons, particularly in reference to semesters, unless followed by a specific year and the word "Semester." _fall semester, fall 2026, Fall 2026 Semester; this winter was cold but the spring should be warmer; UA students will compete in the Summer Olympics_.[^1]

[^1]: Strategic Communications. See ["Start smart and stay safe in fall 2026"](https://news.ua.edu/2026/08/start-smart-and-stay-safe-in-fall-2026/) (headline and first paragraph) for usage examples.
