---
title: microcertificate, micro-
section: Word Choice
---
In general, no hyphen after this prefix, e.g., _microcertificate, microcredential, microeconomics, micromanage_.[^1] Use a hyphen before a capitalized word or when needed to avoid an awkward double vowel, such as _micro-organism_. Apply this pattern to UA Online marketing terms such as _microcertificate_ and _microcredential_.

[^1]: [https://www.apstylebook.com/ap\_stylebook/prefixes](https://www.apstylebook.com/ap_stylebook/prefixes)
